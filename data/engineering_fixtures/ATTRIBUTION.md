# Previously completed engineering fixture

`learning_ablation_tiny_v1.zip` preserves an already completed development run
with seeds 17/18, eight trials per phase and eight evaluation cases. It contains
138 original primary files, their artifact inventory and independent verification,
and the original external registration and configuration: 142 unchanged files.
The adjacent manifest records every original path, byte count and SHA-256, the
archive SHA-256 and the original local copy location. ZIP entry timestamps are
fixed at 2000-01-01 00:00:00 for deterministic packaging.

Packaging performed no new learning, learner initialization, data generation or
random draws. The fixture is engineering evidence, not another scientific run,
an animal experiment or a human dataset. Its original verification report and
registration bytes remain unchanged. Tests copy these existing states and create
clearly marked synthetic documentation links only in temporary directories.

The structural artifacts and network states derive from the bundled FlyWire FAFB
v783 KC→MBON projection. Preserve the attribution and **CC BY-NC 4.0** data notice
in [data/LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md), the exact upstream source/version information
in [data/source-manifest.json](../source-manifest.json), and the projection's
[provenance.json](../flywire_783_mb_left_kc512_s701/provenance.json). The extract was
reused unchanged from DrosoMem commit `7fb156c193c7a499fc2266a164b705729abbab21`.
The data notice cites Dorkenwald et al. (2024), Schlegel et al. (2024), Shiu et al.
(2024) and the pinned upstream connectivity/annotation repositories.
